# Lithuania's best finance seats hide in small teams

For a Lithuanian HEC Paris MSc International Finance graduate who wants to come home, the strongest combination of prestige, pay and fit sits in a handful of small private-markets and trading teams in Vilnius. **INVL Asset Management** is the deepest PE employer, with a record **EUR 410M buyout fund** and a new **EUR 200M CEE bank fund** launched on 9 October 2026. **Covalis Capital**, a London hedge fund founded by a Palanga-born CIO, quietly runs an equity research team in Vilnius and is the most prestigious public-markets seat in the country. Next come the pan-Baltic buyout houses **BaltCap** and **Livonia Partners**, the M&A boutique **Oaklins Baltics**, and **Ignitis Group**, which runs Lithuania's only real energy trading desk and has the highest pay median among state-owned firms. Two quant shops show up only in social-insurance (Sodra) data: **Humbility** (crypto HFT) and **VNT Technologies**. They pay the most in the country for juniors, but they hire coders and olympiad mathematicians rather than finance master's graduates. Expect a realistic starting salary of about **EUR 2,000 to 3,500 gross per month** in most fields. Pay goes higher only in quant trading, where VNT advertises **EUR 5,000 to 10,000** base. The entity averages of EUR 5,000 to 7,000 seen at PE and real estate managers include partners and say little about junior pay. Most figures below came from search-engine summaries of aggregator pages because direct Lithuanian sources were blocked, so every number needs checking before you rely on it.

Just outside the list are Eika Asset Management and Capitalica (real estate managers with EUR 5,000 to 6,900 averages), Nasdaq Vilnius and Danske Bank (clearing-risk and model-validation quants), Vinted (treasury and FP&A ads up to EUR 4,650), Superia (M&A boutique), the Bank of Lithuania and ILTE (institutions), and INVL Family Office and Tesonet's investment unit.

**INVL ranks first** because it combines scale, breadth and real junior seats. Its second-generation PE fund closed above its hard cap at a **record EUR 410M** ([INVL](https://www.invl.com/en/news/invalda-invl-group-second-generation-pe-fund-closes-above-hard-cap-with-record-eur-410-million)). Its fund page lists associates and junior analysts ([INVL PE Fund II](https://invl.com/en/investments/invl-private-equity-fund-ii/)). The group also runs private debt, renewables and real estate. Group AUM passed **EUR 2.3bn** in August 2026 ([Invalda INVL](https://invaldainvl.com/)). It has just launched a closed-end fund of up to **EUR 200M** to buy stakes in CEE banks ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/09/3377823/0/en/a-new-invl-fund-of-up-to-eur-200-million-will-invest-in-central-and-eastern-european-banks.html)). A new fund in its first-close phase is the classic moment for a team to add analysts. The realistic entry point is one step from the deal team: an Investment Valuation Analyst role advertised at **EUR 2,900 to 3,500 plus bonus**, which asks for two years in audit, transaction advisory or valuation ([cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/investiciju-vertinimo-analitikas-e-vilniuje/1-14034448)).

**Covalis ranks second, not first,** because prestige is not the same as opportunity. It is the only foreign hedge fund confirmed to have investment staff in Vilnius. Equity analysts there have been on staff since 2021 and 2022 ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rokas-jusys-2112471b2/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vaidas-ali%C5%A1auskas-b866b9193/)). The fund covers about 300 companies across about 30 countries in utilities, infrastructure, renewables, industrials and materials ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/covalis-capital-returns-aum-holdings-191754835.html)), which fits a finance master's graduate well. However, it has no visible Lithuanian legal entity, no job ads and no pay data, and the Vilnius team is probably a handful of people. You get in by approaching the current analysts and the London office directly.

**BaltCap and Livonia** are the prestige buyout names, but their Vilnius teams are small. They usually hire associates with one to two years of Big 4 transaction services or IB experience, not fresh graduates. **Oaklins** is the most consistent Baltic M&A boutique, but it hires about one analyst a year. **Ignitis ranks high on accessibility**: it posts many salaried trading, treasury and M&A roles and runs a young-talents programme. Its pay median beats almost every bank. **Humbility and VNT pay the most** but rank lower because their hiring profile (olympiad, Oxbridge maths, PhDs, production code) is not what an HEC MIF degree signals. If you code seriously in Python or C++, move them up to the top five.

## Private markets give an HEC graduate the deepest bench

Lithuania's alternative-asset industry is small but deep enough to build a career in, and it pays better than its profile suggests. Baltic managers raised about **EUR 749M in 2025**, including a **record EUR 502M for PE**, and there were 116 active Baltic PE and VC funds at end-2025 ([KPMG/Baltic VCAs](https://kpmg.com/ee/en/insights/2026/06/baltic-private-equity-and-venture-capital-funds-raised-nearly--7.html)). Four PE houses are confirmed to have investment staff in Vilnius: INVL, BaltCap, Livonia and Lords LB.

**BaltCap** has 35+ investment professionals across six cities, including Vilnius ([BaltCap LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/baltcap)). It runs a **EUR 200M infrastructure fund** anchored by the EIF and Baltic pension funds ([BaltCap](https://www.baltcap.com/baltcap-launches-e200-million-successor-infrastructure-fund/)). Its Lithuanian entity shows **8 staff averaging about EUR 5,000 to 5,650** ([okredo](https://okredo.com/lt-lt/imone/baltcap-uab-111764858); [Scoris](https://scoris.lt/en/imone/111764858)). There is a real reputational issue: the EPPO is investigating a former Lithuanian partner of the infrastructure fund over alleged misappropriation of more than EUR 42M, and widened the investigation in March 2025 ([EPPO](https://www.eppo.europa.eu/en/media/news/lithuania-investigation-baltcap-infrastructure-fund-widens)). Check the case status before accepting an offer, and expect interviewers to discuss it. **Livonia Partners** has a Vilnius office and is raising **Fund III**. The EBRD approved up to EUR 30M toward a EUR 225M total in May 2026 ([EBRD](https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/56209.html)), and the fund is preparing a first close that would make it Livonia's largest ([Real Deals](https://realdeals.eu.com/article/exclusive-baltic-pe-firm-livonia-prepares-first-close-on-fund-iii)). The big CEE houses (Enterprise Investors, MidEuropa, Abris) do Lithuanian deals but staff them from Warsaw or London. Lithuanians who want those firms have to join there.

Real estate and private debt managers are the hidden high payers of Lithuanian finance. **Lords LB** (EUR 1.48bn AUM, 36 staff) shows entity averages of **EUR 6,068 to 7,229** ([okredo](https://okredo.com/lt-lt/imone/uab-lords-lb-asset-management-301849625); [Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/301849625)). **Eika Asset Management** (19 to 20 staff) shows EUR 4,972 to 6,853 ([fsaskaita](https://fsaskaita.lt/imone/uab-eika-asset-management-304094078); [okredo](https://okredo.com/lt-lt/imone/uab-eika-asset-management-304094078)). **Capitalica** reported EUR 6,625 in its 2023 annual report with 9 staff ([Capitalica](https://capitalica.lt/app/uploads/2024/06/Capitalica-Asset-Management-2023-m.-finansine-ataskaita.pdf)). These averages run roughly double the finance-sector average of about EUR 3,000 to 4,000 ([dirba.lt](https://www.dirba.lt/straipsniai/atlyginimai-lietuvoje-2026-minimali-alga-vidutinis-616.html)), but in teams this small they mostly reflect partners. In private credit, **Nter Asset Management** runs what it calls Lithuania's largest private debt fund and lists an Investment Associate by name ([Nter AM](https://www.nteram.lt/en/leading-baltic-private-debt-fund)). That is evidence that junior credit seats exist.

Venture capital is prestigious but thin. **Contrarian Ventures** (pan-European climate tech, with a Fund II of about EUR 100M anchored by EUR 25M from the EIF) is the most international name ([EIF](https://www.eif.org/press/all/investeu-eif-invests-eur-25-million-into-pan-european-climate-tech-venture-capital-fund-contrarian-ventures)). **Practica Capital** closed an **EUR 80M seed fund** at its hard cap ([Practica](https://practica.vc/en/news/practica-capital-closes-eur80-million-fund-committed-to-early-stage-investments-in-the-baltics)). Practica's Sodra figures swing from about EUR 4,300 to EUR 11,968 in a single December, which points to partner distributions rather than analyst pay ([imoniuinformacija](https://imoniuinformacija.lt/en/company/practica-capital-management-uab/)). Iron Wolf's average of about EUR 2,100 shows the other extreme ([Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/304711768)). The state development bank ILTE keeps funding new managers, including a EUR 40M defence programme opened in May 2026 ([BNS](https://sc.bns.lt/view/item/ilte-iesko-valdytoju-naujai-18-mln-eur-rizikos-kapitalo-priemonei-501241)). New fund teams are a realistic entry point, and Coinvest Capital's Investment Manager post started at **EUR 3,300** ([Coinvest](https://www.coinvest.lt/post/join-our-team-as-an-investment-manager)).

## Hedge fund and quant seats exist but are hard to find

No global multi-manager hedge fund or major HFT firm (Millennium, Citadel, Optiver, IMC, Jane Street, Jump, DRW) was found in Vilnius, and no MBB strategy firm either. Lithuania's public-markets and trading talent is concentrated in four places: Covalis, two home-grown quant firms, and Ignitis.

**Covalis Capital** is the model of the hard-to-find employer. Founder Zach Mecelis was born in Palanga and launched the fund in 2012 after GLG and Noble Group ([Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Mecelis)). In 2023 Covalis ran an activist director slate at Enel ([Startmag](https://www.startmag.it/economia/tutti-i-perche-del-flop-di-mecelis-covalis-nellassemblea-enel/)). Its LinkedIn page lists Vilnius as a location ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/covalis-capital-llp)), but registry searches found no "Covalis" UAB. The Vilnius analysts are probably employed through the UK LLP or as contractors, which is why the firm never appears in Sodra rankings. One aggregator profile suggests a former Covalis Vilnius analyst now heads financial advisory at Grant Thornton Lithuania ([RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/mantas-gilius-email_161979142)). That is unverified, but it hints that the seat leads to good exits. Other London or New York funds may also run quiet Vilnius research teams. Nobody searched LinkedIn for "Vilnius plus hedge fund analyst" systematically, so that search is worth doing by hand.

**Humbility, UAB** calls itself a proprietary HFT firm trading across centralised and decentralised digital-asset markets. Its team includes Cambridge, Imperial, Princeton and Oxford graduates, and it pays annual profit sharing ([humbility.io](https://humbility.io/)). Its Sodra averages ran **about EUR 6,100 to 6,300** in late 2025 and early 2026, with one month near EUR 40,000 that was probably a profit-share payout ([Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/305825881/darbuotojai_atlyginimai)). **VNT Technologies, UAB** advertises a quant researcher role at **EUR 5,000 to 10,000 base plus a year-end bonus** and says finance experience is not required ([VNT](https://vnttechnologies.com/quantitative-researcher)). That is the highest posted quant range found in Lithuania. Its 2025 revenue was about EUR 3.7M with about EUR 1.6M profit, per one aggregator ([okredo](https://okredo.com/lt-lt/imone/uab-vnt-technologies-306700560)). Both firms register as "computer programming", not finance, which is why searches filtered on finance activity codes miss them. The nearest large crypto market maker, Gravity Team, is in Riga ([Gravity Team](https://www.gravityteam.co/careers)).

**Ignitis Group** is the energy-trading anchor and the most accessible trading employer. Its Vilnius desk covers day-ahead, intraday, hedging, gas and LNG, and it has advertised a Chief Trading Officer role ([Ignitis](https://ignitisgrupe.lt/en/job-offers/chief-trading-officer-fmd-2287)). Recent salaried ads include Market Analyst for Power Trading at **EUR 3,430 to 5,150** ([cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/market-analyst-power-trading-vilniuje/1-14112520)) and Senior Quantitative Researcher at **EUR 3,430 to 5,150 plus variable pay** ([beBee](https://bebee.com/lt/jobs/senior-quantitative-researcher-ignitis-lietuva-vilnius--techmap_lt_4444418533)). The Hedge Power Analyst ad stresses learning trading tools, which suggests a junior-friendly role ([Built In](https://builtin.com/job/hedge-power-analyst-fmd/3458918)). In September 2026 Ignitis announced it would enter a long-term LNG purchase agreement ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/10/3359205/0/en/ignitis-to-enter-into-a-long-term-lng-purchase-agreement.html)), so the trading function is growing. No foreign trading house (Vitol, Trafigura, Statkraft, Axpo, Danske Commodities) was found with a Lithuanian desk, and Inter RAO Lietuva is under sanctions and should be avoided ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2913025/inter-rao-lietuvos-pertvarka-nesankcionuoti-akcininkai-ketina-kurti-nauja-imone)). Smaller quant seats include TransferGo's FX and Treasury desk ([Greenhouse](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/transfergo/jobs/6005765004)) and SEB's credit-risk model validation team at **EUR 3,750 to 5,650** ([cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/quantitative-analyst-in-credit-risk-model-validation-seb-vilnius-vilniuje/1-14116548?from=archived_ad_redirect_404)).

## Advisory, banks and institutions trade pay for structure

M&A advisory in Vilnius is prestigious but small. In 2024 the Baltic adviser table by deal count was **PwC 8, Oaklins 6, Superia 5** ([PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ee/en/press-room/news-and-articles/PwCranked1BalticandGlobalMAAdvisorbyVolumefor2024.html)). Superia claims first place for 2025 ([Superia](https://www.superia.lt/news/superia-ranked-1-ma-adviser-baltics-number-transactions)), and KPMG won Mergermarket's Baltic award for 2023 ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/lt/en/insights/2023/12/kpmg-recognized-by-mergermarket-as-the-leading-financial-adviser.html)). Oaklins' Lithuanian office was co-founded in 2013 by a former Swedbank investment banker and reached first place in the market by volume ([Oaklins](https://www.oaklins.com/ie/en/team/7839/)). It hired an analyst in Vilnius in 2025 ([Oaklins](https://www.oaklins.com/baltics/en/team/24033/)). Superia's Vilnius entity has about 5 staff averaging about EUR 2,262 ([Scoris](https://scoris.lt/en/imone/135215985)), a reminder that boutique prestige does not mean high pay. The Big 4 deal teams are the structured entry route. EY's junior Strategy and Transactions consultant role started from **EUR 1,400** ([cvonline](https://www.cvonline.lt/lt/vacancy/888481/ey-ernst-and-young-baltic-uab/junior-strategy-and-transactions-consultant)), and PwC's senior consultant in Deals pays **EUR 2,500 to 4,000** ([cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/senior-consultant-in-deals-vilniuje/1-11489670)). Two years in a Big 4 deals team is also the standard way into BaltCap, Livonia and INVL associate seats.

Bank CIB in Vilnius means corporate lending, credit analysis, and project and leveraged finance, not bulge-bracket ECM or M&A execution. SEB's Vilnius project finance team covers leveraged finance, fund financing, PPP and renewables ([SEB](https://jobs.eu.lever.co/seb/e5529a61-aa06-466d-baaf-da9eced457b4)). SEB and Swedbank pay averages of about **EUR 3,700 to 3,830** ([pinokis SEB](https://pinokis.com/atsiliepimai/imone/SEB+Bankas/51); [pinokis Swedbank](https://pinokis.com/atsiliepimai/imone/Swedbank_Swedbank%2C%20AB/66096)). **Luminor** is being sold by Blackstone and DNB to OTP Bank under an agreement signed on 20 July 2026 ([DNB](https://ir.dnb.no/press-and-reports/press-releases/dnb-bank-asa-agreement-divest-shareholding-luminor-holding-2470246)). That creates uncertainty there through 2027. **Revolut Bank UAB** is the best-paying bank entity: about **EUR 5,500 to 5,800** across about 110 to 130 staff ([tax.lt](https://tax.lt/imones/101691-revolut-bank-uab); [1551.lt](https://www.1551.lt/revolut-bank-uab-224625/)). That entity holds the senior risk, treasury and regulatory staff, and its EU Bank Portfolio Manager role was advertised at **EUR 3,800 to 4,200** ([LinkedIn](https://lt.linkedin.com/jobs/investment-manager-jobs?countryRedirected=1)).

Global hubs and corporates offer volume and structure. **Danske Bank** employs about 4,400 to 4,600 people in Vilnius at an average of about **EUR 4,037** ([4w.lt](https://4w.lt/imone/301694694/darbuotojai)). It runs model-validation quant roles and the FuturePros graduate programme, after which 80% of participants stay on ([Danske](https://danskebank.lt/en/career/future-pros)). **Nasdaq Vilnius Services** (about 550 to 600 staff, about EUR 4,000 to 4,450) hires clearing-risk quants and issuer surveillance analysts ([tax.lt](https://tax.lt/imones/97526-nasdaq-vilnius-services-uab); [jobsinforex](https://www.jobsinforex.com/job/176363/issuer-surveillance-analyst/)). **Ignitis** posted a group median of **EUR 5,802** in July 2026, the highest among state-owned firms ([TV3](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/ispudingi-atlyginimai-valstybes-imonese-daugiau-nei-puse-darbuotoju-uzdirba-ir-5800-euru-n1555178)), and runs Group Treasury and IR teams. **Vinted** advertises treasury and FP&A roles at **EUR 3,233 to 4,650** ([Insight Partners](https://jobs.insightpartners.com/companies/vinted/jobs/62925404-senior-treasury-analyst); [Vinted](https://careers.vinted.com/jobs/j/4848497101)). Fund administrators such as Citco and Alter Domus are growing in Vilnius, but they offer operations roles, with a Citco trade-matching job at EUR 2,000 to 2,500 ([MeetFrank](https://meetfrank.com/finance-jobs-in-lithuania)).

The bank-owned pension managers pay well but are tiny. Luminor's investment management arm had 11 staff averaging **EUR 5,138** in 2024 ([Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/226299280)), and Swedbank's had 14 at about EUR 3,800 to 4,000 ([info.lt](https://www.info.lt/imones/Swedbank-investicij%C5%B3-valdymas-UAB/2106256)). Among institutions, the **Bank of Lithuania** is the most accessible high-signal option. Its internships are open to master's students and paid EUR 1,160 in 2026 ([karjera.lb.lt](https://karjera.lb.lt/jobs/7591294-stazuote-investicines-ir-kriptoturto-imones-bei-ju-rinkos)), and division heads average about EUR 6,536 ([LB](https://www.lb.lt/lt/informacija-apie-darbo-uzmokesti)). **ILTE**, the national development bank, signed about **EUR 1.3bn** of contracts in 2025, roughly four times its 2024 volume ([ILTE](https://ilte.lt/data/public/uploads/2026/04/en_ilte-consolidated-management-report-and-financial-statements-for-2025.pdf)). It is a growing employer for fund-of-funds and securitisation work, though no pay data was found.

## Sodra averages mislead unless you read headcount

Every Lithuanian employer's average insured wage is public, which makes Sodra data a powerful screening tool and also an easy one to misread. The averages cover the whole entity: partners, directors, back office, and bonus or carry months. In a team of eight, one partner's distribution can double the figure. The averages are gross, while many Lithuanian headlines quote net pay. At average wages, gross is about 1.6 times net: **EUR 2,480 gross versus EUR 1,514 net** in Q4 2025 ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2849151/sodra-gyventojai-lietuvoje-vidutiniskai-uzdirba-1-514-euru-i-rankas)). For scale, the national gross average was about **EUR 2,628** in Q2 2026 ([tagidas](https://www.tagidas.lt/savadai/9006/)).

The extreme finance figures are traps. On a Sodra-based top-100 list, **GPS Capital Markets Europe UAB** shows an average of **EUR 55,828 with 4 employees**, **AlphaPay UAB** EUR 15,693 with 6, and **Kevin EU UAB** EUR 13,966 ([rekvi.lt](https://rekvi.lt/en/top100?sort=atlyginimai)). The first two reflect one or two executives in tiny entities and say nothing about hiring. Kevin is worse: its company, kevin., entered insolvency in September 2024 and lost its payment-institution licence ([Bank of Lithuania](https://www.lb.lt/en/news/payment-institution-licence-of-kevin-eu-uab-revoked)). Binance's Lithuanian arm, Bifinity, is likewise down to one employee ([LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/2957111/binance-withdraws-from-lithuania)). Treat any average above EUR 10,000 in an entity of fewer than 15 people as noise until a job ad or an employee confirms it. Sodra also publishes nothing for entities with three or fewer insured staff, so the smallest GP vehicles are invisible.

This report also has a data limitation of its own. The research environment's proxy blocked atvira.sodra.lt, rekvizitai.vz.lt, lb.lt, cvbankas.lt and most firm websites. As a result, **most figures here come from search-engine summaries of aggregator pages** (okredo, Scoris, tax.lt, info.lt, rekvi.lt and others), not from reading the pages in full. Aggregators disagree by month and method, and several job ads are more than a year old. Verify every number on rekvizitai.vz.lt or atvira.sodra.lt, and check job ads on cvbankas.lt before you rely on them. To find more hidden payers like Humbility and VNT, run the filter script at `research_notes/Lithuania high finance employers/raw/sodra_filter_finance.py` on the monthly per-employer Sodra file. **The script is untested and its download URL is unverified.** It filters for finance codes (EVRK 64, 65, 66, 70.22, 46.12, 35.14) above EUR 5,000. Because both quant shops register as programming firms, add EVRK 62.10 for small entities as well, then screen out payments and crypto licence shells. A companion Excel workbook with the employer data is being built separately at `/home/user/Claude/reports/Lithuania high finance employers.xlsx`.

## Networks and fund cycles decide who gets hired

None of the top-ranked firms runs campus recruiting in Lithuania. Analyst intake at boutiques and PE houses is zero to two people a year, and the quant firms hire through their own sites, LinkedIn and quant job boards ([MeetFrank](https://meetfrank.com/company/humbility)). Fund cycles are the most useful timing signal. INVL's bank fund targets a first close by end-2026, and Livonia's Fund III is near its first close, so both are likely to add juniors in 2026 and 2027. The diaspora networks help with access more than with pay. Global Lithuanian Leaders has 10,000+ members, holds its awards on 21 December 2026 ([GLL](https://lithuanianleaders.org/)), and runs the LT Big Brother mentoring programme with Lithuanian professionals. The LT VCA brings together 64 PE and VC members ([vca.lt](https://www.vca.lt/)). Kurk Lietuvai places returnees in public-sector projects, which builds ties with the Ministry of Finance and the Bank of Lithuania at public-sector pay ([Kurk Lietuvai](https://www.kurklt.lt/en)).

## Conclusion

Lithuanian high finance is not one ladder but a set of separate small rooms. The best-paid ones (quant shops, real estate managers, the Covalis research desk) are almost invisible in standard searches because they register under misleading activity codes, operate through foreign entities, or never post ads. That gives a determined returnee an advantage: a direct approach to a five-person team, backed by an HEC degree, beats an application into a pipeline that does not exist. The most robust plan is to target INVL, Ignitis or a Big 4 deals team as a structured first seat and to cultivate Covalis, BaltCap, Livonia and Oaklins in parallel. Use the Sodra filter to find the next Humbility.

The pay ceiling is real, and it argues for sequencing rather than giving up. Junior pay at even the best Vilnius employers sits around EUR 2,000 to 3,500 gross, a fraction of London or Paris. The entity averages of EUR 5,000 to 7,000 show what senior people earn after a few years, not what graduates start on. A graduate who can start abroad in IB, PE or energy trading and return with two or three years of experience will enter the Lithuanian market at the associate level, where scarcity works in their favour. A graduate who wants to return immediately should weigh the INVL and Ignitis pipelines above the higher-profile but nearly closed doors.
