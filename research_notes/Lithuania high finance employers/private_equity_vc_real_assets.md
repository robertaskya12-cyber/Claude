## Q1. Baltic private equity / buyout / growth: which firms actually have investment staff in Vilnius?

### Takeaway
Four PE houses are confirmed to have investment staff in Vilnius: INVL Asset Management (by far the largest Lithuanian PE employer, with a €410M Fund II and a new €200M bank fund in October 2026), BaltCap (Vilnius office, part of a 35+ professional pan-Baltic team), Livonia Partners (Vilnius office, partner Mindaugas Utkevičius, raising Fund III) and Lords LB (mostly real estate). Several firms named in the brief have no confirmed Vilnius investment team: the large CEE houses (Enterprise Investors, Abris, MidEuropa, Resource Partners) and "Provenance Capital". They do deals in Lithuania but staff them from Warsaw or London.

### Inferences
- For a candidate who wants a Vilnius-based PE seat, INVL is the deepest employer by far: about 90 staff, a €410M buyout fund, a €200M bank fund being raised, plus infrastructure, private debt and real estate. BaltCap and Livonia are the prestige pan-Baltic buyout names, but each Vilnius team is probably only a handful of professionals.
- Livonia Fund III and INVL's bank fund are both raising in late 2026. Teams usually add junior capacity after a first close, so 2026–27 openings are plausible.
- The BaltCap EPPO case could be asked about in interviews and may weigh on BaltCap's Lithuanian infrastructure franchise. Check its status before recommending the firm.
- The big CEE funds (MidEuropa, Enterprise, Abris) recruit Lithuanians into Warsaw or London, not Vilnius.

## Q2. Venture capital: Lithuanian-founded or Baltic-focused funds and national fund-of-funds

### Takeaway
Vilnius has a dense layer of small VC managers (Practica, Iron Wolf, Contrarian Ventures, Coinvest, FIRSTPICK, Open Circle, Katalista, Baltic Sandbox, Lords LB VC), many of them anchored by ILTE (the national development bank, successor to INVEGA's venture role) and the EIF. Teams typically have 3–10 people. Entity-level Sodra averages run about €2,000–5,500, with Practica Capital Management a possible outlier on the high side. Change Ventures and Superangel are Estonian, with at most a Lithuanian associate.

### Inferences
- **The "hidden" high payers are Lords LB and Eika Asset Management.** Both are real estate managers with 20–36 staff averaging €5–7k, comparable to or above INVL and BaltCap, despite a lower prestige profile among finance students. Capitalica is similar but smaller.
- INVL's about €5.1–5.6k average across about 90 people (including back office and retail asset management) implies investment-team pay well above that average.
- Small VC averages are distorted by partner payouts (Practica) or by junior and part-time staff (Iron Wolf). They say little about analyst pay.
- Averages include partners, so junior pay is probably €2–3.5k, in line with the job ads in Q5.
