Research method note: all findings come from web search result snippets and summaries. Direct page fetches (superia.lt, pwc.com, sorainen.com, cvbankas.lt, lever.co) failed in this environment because of DNS/proxy blocks, so numbers below are as quoted in search results. ## Q1. M&A / corporate finance boutiques and Big 4 / mid-tier transaction teams: which are active and staff Vilnius?

### Takeaway
The independent M&A advisers with real Vilnius teams are Oaklins Baltics (whose Lithuanian arm appears to be Alpha Advisors UAB, Vilnius, founded 2013, the most consistent Baltic league-table leader), Superia (Superia LT UAB, small Vilnius office of about 5 people; it claims #1 by 2025 Mergermarket deal count), and the Big 4 deal teams (PwC Deals, KPMG Deal Advisory, EY Strategy & Transactions, Deloitte Financial Advisory), plus Grant Thornton Baltic. Orion Securities (licensed broker with a CF arm) and INVL Financial Advisors also have Vilnius operations. I found no evidence of Vilnius teams for Redgate Capital (Tallinn and Riga) or Catella Corporate Finance, and no trace of the "Prudentia", "Avant Advisory" or "Fortuna" boutiques.

### Takeaway

## Q5. Pay levels and entry paths for a master's graduate

### Takeaway
Vilnius deal and advisory pay for entry to junior roles is roughly €1,400–2,500 gross per month. Senior consultant and senior associate roles pay about €2,500–4,000, and bank CIB analyst or officer roles about €1,950–2,800. Treasury/ALM at Revolut paid about €3,600–4,200. Entry paths are Big 4 internships and trainee intakes, bank CIB analyst roles, and networking into boutique analyst seats (1–2 a year).

### Cited Findings
- EY Junior S&T Consultant from €1,400 — [cvonline](https://www.cvonline.lt/lt/vacancy/888481/ey-ernst-and-young-baltic-uab/junior-strategy-and-transactions-consultant); KPMG Deal Advisory Senior Analyst €1,900–2,500 — [cvbankas](https://ua.cvbankas.lt/senior-analyst-in-deal-advisory-vilniuje/1-13680555); KPMG Senior €2,500–3,500 — [cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/senior-in-deal-advisory-team-vilniuje/1-11236906); PwC Senior Consultant Deals €2,500–4,000 — [cvbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/senior-consultant-in-deals-vilniuje/1-11489670); Deloitte FA Senior Associate €2,000–2,900 (2022) — [ACCA](https://jobs.accaglobal.com/job/11493336/senior-associate-at-financial-department/)
- Superia LT average salary about €2,262 (about 5 staff) — [Scoris](https://scoris.lt/en/imone/135215985); KPMG Baltics average €3,711–3,864 (2026) — [info.lt](https://www.info.lt/imones/KPMG-Baltics-UAB/2111159)
- Oaklins hired an analyst in Vilnius in 2025. — [Oaklins profile](https://www.oaklins.com/baltics/en/team/24033/)

### Inferences
- An HEC MSc International Finance graduate would likely enter at the top of junior bands (about €2,000–2,800 gross) at the Big 4 or a bank. Boutique seats are scarce. For "high-paying prestigious" goals, Vilnius tops out well below Western European IB, so a common path is to start in London, Paris or the Nordics and return at a senior level.

### Gaps
- Glassdoor Vilnius data, Big 4 deals graduate-programme specifics and boutique entry salaries were not found.
