# Energy, Power, Gas and Commodity Trading Employers in Lithuania (as of Oct 2026)

**Method note for the report writer:** Direct page fetches were blocked in this research environment for rekvizitai.vz.lt, atvira.sodra.lt, cvbankas.lt, getbaltic.com, ignitisgrupe.lt and builtin.com (DNS failures or proxy 403). Everything below comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not from full pages, so the data is thinner than intended. In particular, current Sodra entity-level salaries could only be found for Elektrum Lietuva, through tax.lt, and company codes could not be checked against the registry. Treat salary bands as "as reported by job-board snippets". Posting dates were often unclear.

## Which utilities run trading desks in Lithuania?

### Takeaway
Ignitis Group is the only utility with a clearly evidenced, actively hiring trading desk in Lithuania (Vilnius). It covers power (day-ahead, intraday, hedging), gas/LNG, quantitative trading analytics and a C-level Chief Trading Officer role. Published gross pay bands for analyst and trader roles run about EUR 3.1k to 5.2k per month. Enefit (Eesti Energia) and Elektrum (Latvenergo) have Vilnius retail and supply subsidiaries, but no evidence was found of trading desks there; group trading appears to sit in Tallinn and Riga. No evidence was found of Axpo, Statkraft or Danske Commodities having Lithuanian trading offices.

### Inferences
- Ignitis' trading pay bands (about EUR 3.1–5.2k gross per month) sit at roughly 1.5–2.5x the Lithuanian average wage. For a Lithuanian candidate wanting a trading career at home, this is the clear anchor employer. Ignitis Gamyba and Ignitis UAB are the likely legal entities, but this was not verified.
- Enefit and Elektrum Vilnius entities look like retail and B2B supply and sales offices. Trading or portfolio roles there, if any, are probably supply-planning or pricing roles rather than proprietary desks.
- The Elektrum Lietuva average (about EUR 3.3k) suggests B2B energy supply pays well above the national average, even without a trading desk.

## Which independent or foreign power/gas/commodity trading firms have Lithuanian entities?

### Takeaway
The searches found no foreign trading house (such as Vitol, Trafigura or Mercuria, or DV Commodities, a large liquidity provider in energy markets) with a Vilnius, Kaunas or Klaipėda trading office. The independent segment found is small: GET Baltic exchange participants such as Baltic Energy Partners OÜ (Estonian), small brokers such as Reservolt UAB, and niche firms like UAB Energy Advice and UAB Bio Energy Trade. Their scale is unverified. Inter RAO Lietuva, formerly the largest independent power importer, is sanctioned and frozen and should be flagged as an exit.

### Cited Findings
- **GET Baltic participants** include Baltic Energy Partners OÜ and Elektrum Lietuva UAB. The full list could not be fetched — [GET Baltic participants](https://www.getbaltic.com/en/trading/exchange-participants/)
- **Reservolt UAB**, Vilnius: registered for electricity trading and as an electricity and gas broker. Scale unconfirmed — [okredo energy companies](https://okredo.com/en-lt/companies/electricity-gas-steam-and-air-conditioning-supply)
- **UAB "Energy Advice"** and **UAB "Bio Energy Trade"** have employer pages on cvbankas. Activity and roles could not be fetched — [Energy Advice on cvbankas](https://en.cvbankas.lt/energy-advice-siulo-darba/4-61709); [Bio Energy Trade on cvbankas](https://en.cvbankas.lt/bio-energy-trade-siulo-darba/4-17846)
- **Inter RAO Lietuva AB (exit/closure flag):**
  - Started in 2002 as Energijos realizacijos centras. Became an independent supplier and exporter in 2003, with Latvian and Estonian subsidiaries from 2010 — [interrao.lt history](https://interrao.lt/en/about-us/history/)
  - Stopped supplying electricity in May 2022 and faces customer lawsuits.
  - Listed by FNTT (the Lithuanian financial crime investigation service) under sanctions over Putin-linked ties. The Supreme Administrative Court referred the case to the EU Court of Justice. The company appealed the FNTT decision again on 20 July 2026.
  - A 2026 spin-off plan would move assets to a new company owned only by the non-sanctioned shareholders, who hold 49%. FNTT approval was not confirmed.
  - Sources: [LRT on spin-off](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2913025/inter-rao-lietuvos-pertvarka-nesankcionuoti-akcininkai-ketina-kurti-nauja-imone); [LRT on court referral](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2486952/teismas-stabdo-fntt-ir-inter-rao-lietuvos-ginca-kreipiasi-i-es-teisingumo-teisma); [interrao.lt reports](https://interrao.lt/en/reports/); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/inter-rao-lietuva/__BNge8kOQiUv8y7etDHei9VPfWmZ20oirDC6Pgv8tXLs)
- **Quant and trading firms in Lithuania:** an aggregator lists 16 "quantitative trading" companies, mostly small software and analytics firms rather than energy houses. Low-quality source — [ensun.io](https://ensun.io/search/quantitative-trading/lithuania)
- **Shipping and agri:**
  - TLC Logistics & Chartering (Klaipėda) charters grain shipments by sea, barge, rail and road — [LinkedIn profile mentioning TLC](https://lt.linkedin.com/in/simas-antuzis-710691181)
  - Birių krovinių terminalas (BKT), a Klaipėda bulk stevedore, has had Belaruskali as a minority owner. Its current status is unverified — [CFTS 2022](https://en.cfts.org.ua/news/one_of_klaipeda_ports_largest_stevedores_offers_its_capacity_for_shipment_of_ukrainian_grain)
- **Advisory ecosystem:** TEGOS law firm runs an energy and natural resources practice in the Baltics, a sign of transactional activity rather than a trading employer — [TEGOS](https://www.tgsbaltic.com/en/practices/regulatory-and-competition/energy/)

### Inferences
- Lithuania's market for trading roles is dominated by one incumbent (Ignitis). The independent trading segment is thin, made up of small suppliers, brokers and aggregators, and foreign desks covering the Baltics mostly operate from Tallinn, Riga, Nordic cities or Warsaw.
- After Russia-linked players left (Inter RAO, plus Gazprom's former stake in Lietuvos Dujos — [Wikipedia: Lietuvos Dujos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lietuvos_Dujos)), the independent import and trading segment shrank further.
- The candidate should treat Akola Group (formerly Linas Agro; grain and agri trading), KN Energies (Klaipėda LNG and oil terminals) and Orlen Lietuva as plausible commodity-adjacent employers. However, no current trading, risk or quant job ads were found for them in this session.

### Gaps
- The full GET Baltic and Nord Pool member lists, and the VERT (regulator) list of electricity and gas supply licences, could not be retrieved. This is the best next step for building an exhaustive list of small traders. Firms to check from general knowledge (unverified this session): Energijos tiekimas UAB, Perlas Energija, Vilniaus energija, Baltpool, Litgrid/EPSO-G (for balancing and market roles), Akola Group, KN Energies, Achema / Achemos grupė, Orlen Lietuva, and Klaipėda carbon or forestry/biomass traders (e.g., Baltpool biofuel exchange participants).
- No carbon or emissions trading firm with a Lithuanian desk was found.
- Sodra averages and headcounts for Reservolt, Energy Advice, Bio Energy Trade and TLC were not obtained.

## Do they hire fresh master's graduates; what are entry roles and paths?

### Takeaway
No dedicated graduate trading programme was found at Ignitis or the other firms. The realistic entry points are Ignitis analyst-level roles: Quantitative Trading Analyst, Hedge Power Analyst, Market Analyst – Power Trading, and the intraday Power Trader-Analyst on evening shifts. Ignitis does run internships, and Ignitis Renewables has a "young talents" page, but these are not trading-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Ignitis Renewables has a "Young talents" page with internship testimonials, but no graduate trading programme is described — [Ignitis Renewables young talents](https://ignitisrenewables.com/young-talents/); [Ignitis Renewables career](https://ignitisrenewables.com/career/)
- Ignitis Group ran a 2026 summer legal internship (Šiauliai), with a stipend of about EUR 650–950 per month as reported. This shows a structured internship channel exists, but not in trading — [legalalphabet](https://legalalphabet.com/internship/legal-intern-corporate-regulatory-ignitis-group-siauliai)
- The Hedge Power Analyst posting emphasises "learning financial trading tools", which suggests a junior-friendly entry role — [builtin.com](https://builtin.com/job/hedge-power-analyst-fmd/3458918)
- The Quantitative Trading Analyst role (forecasting models and reports for the power trading team) is a natural fit for a quant-leaning finance MSc — [cv.lt](https://cv.lt/energetikos-elektronikos-darbai/quantitative-trading-analyst-fmd-vilniuje-1-426005109)

### Inferences
- **Likely path for an HEC MSc International Finance graduate:**
  1. Join as an Ignitis Market or Quant/Hedge analyst.
  2. Move to a day-ahead or intraday trader seat.
  3. Progress to portfolio management, origination (PPAs and LNG), or a move abroad to a Nordic or Western European desk (e.g., Statkraft, Axpo or Danske Commodities in their home hubs).
- Ignitis' new long-term LNG contract (September 2026) and a C-level trading hire suggest the trading function is growing. This is a plausible sign of continued analyst hiring, but it is not confirmed.
- For prestige and pay, the candidate should treat Lithuania-based energy trading as solid but regionally capped. The higher-paying route likely needs a foreign trading hub, with Lithuania as the fallback or return option.

### Gaps
- Requirements in the Ignitis ads (years of experience, degree) could not be read in full, so whether fresh graduates qualify is not confirmed per role.
- No evidence was found of graduate schemes at Enefit, Elektrum, Orlen Lietuva, Achema, Akola Group or KN Energies in this session.
