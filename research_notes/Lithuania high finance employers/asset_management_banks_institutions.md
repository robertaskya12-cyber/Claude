# Lithuania investment-side employers (as of Oct 2026): asset management, pensions, wealth/family offices, bank treasury/ALM, central bank and public/development finance

Research note on method and limits: In this session, every Lithuanian primary source returned a DNS or egress-proxy denial. That covers lb.lt, atvira.sodra.lt, rekvizitai.vz.lt, scoris.lt, artea.lt, invl.com, ilte.lt, cvbankas.lt, vz.lt and delfi.lt. As a result, all findings below come from web-search snippets and summaries of those pages and of aggregators (okredo, info.lt, istaigos.lt, pinokis, infocloud, tax.lt). None of the pages was fetched directly. Figures labelled "Sodra-based" come from aggregators that republish Sodra insured-employee data. Periods and methods differ, so treat comparisons as indicative. For scale: the Lithuanian national average gross wage was EUR 2,628 in Q2 2026, and the 2026 official VDU is EUR 2,312.15 ([tagidas.lt](https://www.tagidas.lt/savadai/9006/); [tagidas VDU](https://www.tagidas.lt/savadai/9029/)).

## Q1. Asset managers and pension fund managers (Bank of Lithuania-licensed management companies)

### Takeaway
The pension/UCITS management market is small and bank-dominated. Six second-pillar managers run about EUR 10.6bn: Swedbank, SEB, Artea, Luminor, Allianz and Goindex. Each investment-management subsidiary has only about 10–15 insured staff. Pay at these subsidiaries is among the highest in Lithuanian finance, with Sodra-based averages of about EUR 3.8k–5.1k/month gross. Headcount is small, so openings are rare. INVL (about EUR 2.3bn group AUM, ~150 people) and Lords LB (about EUR 1.4bn real estate/PE) are the largest independent alternative managers.

### Cited Findings
**Market structure**
- Second-pillar pension fund assets reached about EUR 10.6bn at end-2025. Six companies manage them under Bank of Lithuania supervision: Swedbank investicijų valdymas, SEB investicijų valdymas, Allianz Lietuva gyvybės draudimas, Artea Asset Management, Luminor investicijų valdymas and Goindex — [TV3](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/bankai-ir-ju-pensiju-fondai-slepia-kiek-gyventoju-paprase-grazinti-sukauptus-pinigus-kodel-n1484027); [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2532696/raudonuojanti-birza-veikia-ir-ii-pakopa-kiek-milijonu-isgaravo-nuo-metu-pradzios)
- An earlier source put second-pillar assets at about EUR 10.5bn and third-pillar assets at about EUR 511m — [investuok.eu](https://investuok.eu/pradedantiesiems/kuriam-ii-pakopos-pensiju-fondui-verta-patiketi-savo-pensija/)
- Second-pillar withdrawals are allowed from the start of 2026 to end-2027. One estimate puts potential outflow from people opting out at about EUR 244m, but the banks have not disclosed actual request numbers — [TV3](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/bankai-ir-ju-pensiju-fondai-slepia-kiek-gyventoju-paprase-grazinti-sukauptus-pinigus-kodel-n1484027); [77.lt](https://77.lt/bankai-neatskleidzia-kiek-klientu-nusprende-pasitraukti-is-ii-pensiju-pakopos)

**Job-ad pay evidence**

- Generic benchmark: finance analyst in Vilnius EUR 2,500–4,000 gross/month, and the finance and insurance sector at EUR 3,000–4,000 gross. These are aggregator estimates, not job ads — [kalkis.lt](https://kalkis.lt/miestai/vilnius/); [pinigai360](https://pinigai360.lt/asmeniniai-finansai/atlyginimu-statistika-lietuvoje-2026/)

### Inferences
- The bank-owned investment-management subsidiaries (Luminor, Swedbank, SEB) pay about 1.5–2x the national average, but each has only about 10–15 staff. Graduate intake is probably 0–2 hires a year per firm, mostly junior analyst or risk/compliance roles rather than PM seats.
- Much of the "investment" work for Swedbank and SEB funds is likely done at group level in Stockholm or Tallinn (Baltic investment teams). The Vilnius entities can be smaller than their AUM suggests. This is not verified.
- For a candidate who wants real investing exposure, INVL (PE, private debt, real estate) and Lords LB (real estate/PE) offer more deal-side roles than the pension managers.
- With second-pillar withdrawals open in 2026–27, pension managers may hire cautiously in 2026.

### Gaps
- No full list from the Bank of Lithuania management-company register (lb.lt was blocked). Other managers that may be licensed include Orion Asset Management, Practica Capital, BaltCap, Lewben/LTF-type managers, Prime Investment, Atlas, Integre and others. None was verified this session.
- No AUM breakdown by individual manager for 2026, and no Sodra pay data for Artea Asset Management, INVL Asset Management, Lords LB, Goindex or Orion.
- Unverified: whether SEB's and Swedbank's Lithuanian subsidiaries hold portfolio management mandates locally or only distribute.
- Finasta: its brokerage/asset management business was acquired by Šiaulių bankas years ago and the brand no longer appears active. Not verified this session.

## Q2. Family offices, wealth managers and holding companies with investment teams

### Takeaway
Lithuania has a few multi-family offices: INVL Family Office (>EUR 900m in client assets), Lewben, Genus, Gerovės valdymas and Nordwealth. They are small, boutique and licensed as brokerage or advisory firms. Invalda INVL is the main listed holding company with an investment team. Lithuanian billionaire family offices (for example around Vilniaus prekyba/Maxima owners) hire opaquely, and no public data was found.

### Cited Findings
- INVL Family Office calls itself the leading Baltic multi-family office, with more than EUR 900m in client assets. It holds a Category B financial brokerage licence and is supervised by the Bank of Lithuania — [INVL Family Office](https://www.invlfamilyoffice.com/); [Altss profile](https://altss.com/profile/invl-family-office)
- Lewben has a Vilnius office offering family-office and wealth-management services. The page may be about 5 years old — [Lewben](https://lewben.com/wealth-management/family-office-services/)
- Genus Family Office is a Vilnius-based multi-family office. Its AUM figure is only an aggregator estimate — [Altss](https://altss.com/profile/genus-family-office)
- Gerovės valdymas has run a multi-family office since 2009 under a financial-advisor licence supervised by the Bank of Lithuania — [Gerovės valdymas](https://www.gerovesvaldymas.lt/?lang=en)
- Preqin lists Nordwealth Family Office as a Vilnius multi-family office — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/investor/nordwealth-family-office/391474)
- Amicorp's Vilnius office offers fund administration, family-office and investment-banking services. Alter Domus also hires fund-services staff in Lithuania — [Amicorp](https://amicorp.com/offices/lithuania/); [Alter Domus careers](https://careers.alterdomus.com/en/location/lithuania-jobs/1298/597427/2)
- Invalda INVL is the holding company behind INVL Asset Management and INVL Family Office, and owns 19.94% of Artea Bank — [Invalda INVL](https://invaldainvl.com/); [Wikipedia: Artea Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artea_Bank)

### Inferences
- Family offices in Lithuania are probably too small (a handful of investment professionals each) to run structured graduate programmes. Entry is most likely through networking or analyst internships.
- Fund administrators (Alter Domus, Amicorp) are back- and middle-office routes, not investing roles.

### Gaps
- No verified data on single-family offices of Lithuanian billionaires (for example the Vilniaus prekyba/Maxima owners, the Vičiūnai group, or ME Investicija), including their investment staff or pay.
- No Sodra pay data was retrieved for INVL Family Office, Lewben or Genus.

## Q3. Bank treasury/ALM, markets and risk functions (SEB, Swedbank, Luminor, Artea, Citadele, Revolut Bank UAB)

### Takeaway
The large banks pay about EUR 3.7–3.8k gross/month on average (Sodra-based, 2026). Revolut Bank UAB's Lithuanian entity shows a higher average (about EUR 6.2k), but those data are dated and cover a small headcount. Graduate programmes in the Baltics are mostly tech (SEB) or group-wide analyst schemes (Swedbank). No dedicated Vilnius treasury/ALM graduate programme was found.

### Cited Findings
- SEB bankas: Sodra-based 2026 average EUR 3,830 gross/month. Another page shows EUR 3,789, and the annual figure is EUR 47,090 gross — [pinokis SEB](https://pinokis.com/atsiliepimai/imone/SEB+Bankas/51)
- Swedbank, AB: Sodra-based 2026 average EUR 3,708 gross/month. Another page shows an annual figure of EUR 38,033 — [pinokis Swedbank](https://pinokis.com/atsiliepimai/imone/Swedbank_Swedbank%2C%20AB/66096)
- Revolut Bank UAB (code 304580906): 93 employees and an average gross salary of EUR 6,211/month, from a page about 2 years old — [Infocloud](https://infocloud.lt/imone/revolut-bank/). Another listing gives 122 employees and an average of EUR 5,744.29, with an unclear entity scope — [Work in Lithuania](https://jobs.workinlithuania.com/companies/55-revolut)
- Revolut Bank UAB is licensed by the ECB and supervised by the Bank of Lithuania, at Konstitucijos pr. 21B, Vilnius — [Revolut careers LT](https://www.revolut.com/en-LT/careers/); [LEI record](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/485100CNU7I70EAPX984)
- Revolut EU Bank "Portfolio Manager" role in Lithuania advertised at EUR 3,800–4,200/month — [LinkedIn](https://lt.linkedin.com/jobs/investment-manager-jobs?countryRedirected=1)
- SEB Tech Trainee Programme: 6 months, starts in September, up to 20 trainees from Sweden and the Baltics, for graduates within the last 2 years. 2026–27 Vilnius intake was posted in June 2026 — [SEB](https://sebgroup.com/career/graduates-and-students/tech-trainee-programme); [MeetFrank](https://meetfrank.com/jobs/seb-lt/seb-tech-trainee-programme-2026-27-junior-qa-engineer-seb-vilnius)
- Swedbank Analyst Development Trainee Programme: about 10 months of rotations. One alumnus became an analyst in Group Treasury. Swedbank takes about 200 trainees a year for 2–12 months, mostly in IT & Data and Finance & Analysis. The programme page is group/Stockholm-oriented — [Swedbank graduate](https://www.swedbank.com/work-with-us/students-and-graduates/swedbank-analyst-development-trainee-program.html); [Swedbank students](https://www.swedbank.com/work-with-us/students-and-graduates.html)
- Swedbank Lithuania advertises junior specialist roles in Private Financial Advice in Vilnius, an entry route to wealth management — [Swedbank jobs](https://jobs.swedbank.com/jobs/28167-jaunesnysis-ioji-specialistas-e-privaciu-finansiniu-konsultaciju-skyriuje-vilniuje)
- Luminor's Vilnius hiring is concentrated in finance, risk, IT and data — [workwork.lt](https://workwork.lt/blog/luminor-lithuania-career); [MeetFrank Luminor](https://meetfrank.com/company/luminor-group-5)
- Artea Bank (formerly Šiaulių bankas) had total assets of about EUR 5.84bn in 2025 and runs its own treasury and asset-management arms — [Wikipedia: Artea Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artea_Bank)

### Inferences
- Large-bank averages of about EUR 3.7–3.8k include many retail and operations staff. Treasury, ALM and markets specialists likely earn above that average, but no direct evidence was found.
- Revolut Bank UAB is plausibly the top-paying bank entity per employee in Lithuania, and its Vilnius treasury, credit-risk and finance teams are the most "international" bank route. The headcount and salary data are dated and may cover a narrow entity.
- SEB and Swedbank group programmes (Stockholm-run, with Baltic placements) are the most structured graduate routes into treasury.

### Gaps
- Sodra data not retrieved for Luminor Bank AS Lithuanian branch, Artea bankas or Citadele (Lithuanian branch). Their company codes were not verified this session.
- No Vilnius-specific treasury, ALM or markets job ads with salary ranges were found. Lithuanian job ads must legally show pay ranges, but cvbankas.lt and cv.lt were not reachable.
- Revolut Bank UAB's current (2026) headcount and the size of its Vilnius treasury/ALM team were not found.

## Q4. Public institutions: Bank of Lithuania, Ministry of Finance, ILTE/INVEGA, EIB/EBRD, ECB

### Takeaway
The Bank of Lithuania (about 677 staff) is the most accessible prestigious public employer. It runs paid 2–3 month internships open to master's students (about EUR 1,160 gross/month in 2026). Its specialist pay bands are low, but senior bands are competitive. ILTE, the national development bank, grew to a EUR 1.5bn portfolio after a 2025 consolidation; per one source it is the former INVEGA. EIB has a Baltic office in Vilnius. ECB traineeships pay EUR 1,170–2,120.

### Cited Findings
**Bank of Lithuania (Lietuvos bankas)**
- 677 employees at end-2024 and 12.3% staff turnover. The PDF text was garbled, so the figure needs checking — [LB Annual Report 2024](https://www.lb.lt/uploads/publications/docs/52253_777d08948d50ad9a61c4a7ae23020c1f.pdf)
- Quarterly pay disclosure by position (Q2 2025, gross monthly): board members about EUR 18,733 and division heads about EUR 6,536. The lowest "specialist" grade averaged about EUR 1,627 per the search summary, which looks low and should be checked. There are also senior and mid-level specialist grades. No department breakdown is given — [LB pay information](https://www.lb.lt/lt/informacija-apie-darbo-uzmokesti)
- Internships are open to master's students, bachelor's students from year 2 and integrated-study students. They last 2–3 months, or 2–6 months per another listing. Apply via karjera.lb.lt or by sending a CV to karjera@lb.lt — [LB internships](https://karjera.lb.lt/pages/praktika-ir-stazuotes); [URM diaspora database](https://globalilietuva.urm.lt/kita-informacija/lietuvos-diasporos-jaunimui-skirta-stazuociu-praktikos-pazintiniu-programu-lietuvoje-duomenu-baze/305/praktika-lietuvos-banke/in-55)
- 2026 example: an internship in supervision of investment and crypto-asset firms paid EUR 1,160 gross/month, lasted up to 2 months and had a 28 April 2026 deadline. Summer internships are advertised in spring — [karjera.lb.lt](https://karjera.lb.lt/jobs/7591294-stazuote-investicines-ir-kriptoturto-imones-bei-ju-rinkos)
- The Bank holds a monetary-policy securities portfolio. Market operations and financial stability functions exist, but no staffing figures were found — [LB Annual Report 2024](https://www.lb.lt/uploads/publications/docs/52253_777d08948d50ad9a61c4a7ae23020c1f.pdf); [Financial Stability Review 2024](https://www.lb.lt/uploads/publications/docs/46281_7fa8ea334d45f6857ef40c17b6670084.pdf)

**Ministry of Finance: state debt management**
- Debt management sits in the State Treasury Department (Valstybės iždo departamentas), which has a Borrowing and Investment Division (Skolinimosi ir investavimo skyrius) — [lrvalstybe.lt](https://www.lrvalstybe.lt/kontaktai/valstybes-izdo-departamentas); [finmin.lrv.lt](https://finmin.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys/valstybes-skolos-valdymas/skolinimosi-reglamentavimas/)

**ILTE (national development bank / national promotional institution)**
- Owned 100% by the Ministry of Finance. The governance portal describes ILTE as formerly UAB "Investicijų ir verslo garantijos" (INVEGA's legal name) and as Lithuania's only national development institution. Four organisations were consolidated on 1 August 2025 — [governance.lt](https://governance.lt/en/vvi/ilte/); [ILTE 2025 report](https://ilte.lt/data/public/uploads/2026/04/en_ilte-consolidated-management-report-and-financial-statements-for-2025.pdf)
- 2025: about EUR 1.3bn of signed contracts (about 4x 2024) and a EUR 1.5bn portfolio. Revenue EUR 40.38m and net profit EUR 14.3m (EUR 7.7m in 2024) — [ILTE 2025 report](https://ilte.lt/data/public/uploads/2026/04/en_ilte-consolidated-management-report-and-financial-statements-for-2025.pdf); [governance.lt](https://governance.lt/en/vvi/ilte/)
- Capital-markets activity: Lithuania's first green securitisation (energy-efficiency loans) with EBRD backing, and EUR 112m of top-rated renovation bonds — [EBRD](https://www.ebrd.com/home/news-and-events/news/2025/ebrd-backs-lithuania-s-first-green-securitisation-with-ilte.html); [Baltic Times](https://www.baltictimes.com/the_first_top_credit-rated_bonds_in_the_baltic_states___ilte_raises__112_million_for_renovation/)

**EIB / EBRD / ECB**
- EIB Group regional office for the Baltic states at Liejyklos g. 8, Vilnius, opened in 2020 — [EIB Vilnius](https://www.eib.org/en/contacts/office/lithuania); [EIB Group in Lithuania](https://www.eib.org/en/publications/20230089-the-european-investment-bank-group-in-lithuania)
- ECB traineeships last 3–6 months (extendable) with a grant of EUR 1,170–2,120/month and are open to EU nationals. Banking Supervision traineeships are advertised on the ECB talent portal — [ECB traineeship rules](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/careers/pdf/traineeship_programme.pdf); [ECB talent](https://talent.ecb.europa.eu/careers/JobDetail/Traineeships-in-Banking-Supervision/10836)
- Invest Lithuania (government FDI agency) has pay data on Glassdoor. It is not an investing role — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Invest-Lithuania-Vilnius-Salaries-EI_IE1374119.0,16_IL.17,24_IM1551.htm)

### Inferences
- For an HEC MSc graduate, the Bank of Lithuania internship is a low-pay, high-signal entry point (market operations, financial stability, supervision). Full-time junior specialist pay at the central bank is likely below private-sector bank averages.
- ILTE is growing quickly (4x financing volume), so it is a plausible employer for fund-of-funds, structured finance and securitisation analysts. Pay data are missing.
- The EIB Vilnius office is mainly a liaison/regional office. Investment-officer roles are mostly in Luxembourg.

### Gaps
- No ILTE headcount, salary data or open-role evidence was found. ILTE's 2025 report gave no employee figure in the snippet.
- No confirmation of the full list of four organisations merged into ILTE in August 2025.
- No Ministry of Finance debt-management job ad or pay data for 2026.
- No EBRD Vilnius office was confirmed. EBRD covers Lithuania via deals such as ILTE, but local presence was not verified.

## Q5. Insurance investment teams

### Takeaway
No evidence was found of sizeable in-house investment teams at Lithuanian insurers (ERGO, Compensa, Gjensidige, BTA, Lietuvos draudimas/PZU). Most appear to be branches or subsidiaries of foreign groups whose portfolios are managed at group level. Allianz Lietuva gyvybės draudimas is the exception, because it manages second-pillar pension funds locally.

### Cited Findings

## Q6. Pay levels: who pays the most?

### Takeaway
On the Sodra-based data found, the top payers are Revolut Bank UAB (about EUR 5.7–6.2k average, dated), Luminor investicijų valdymas (EUR 5,138 in 2024) and Swedbank investicijų valdymas (about EUR 3.8–4.0k). The big banks average about EUR 3.7–3.8k. All are 1.4–2.4x the national average of about EUR 2.6k. Public-sector entry pay is lower: Bank of Lithuania internships about EUR 1,160 and ECB traineeships EUR 1,170–2,120.
