# Lithuania high-value finance employers (2026): global financial hubs, fintechs, corporate finance at top corporates

Research date: 2026-10-09. Candidate lens: Lithuanian HEC Paris MSc International Finance student, early career, targeting high-paying, prestigious, high-value (not back-office) finance roles.

Method note: rekvizitai.vz.lt, atvira.sodra.lt and workinlithuania.com could not be fetched directly in this environment. Company-level pay is therefore taken from Sodra-derived aggregator sites (tax.lt, scoris.lt, info.lt, 4w.lt, 1551.lt, topimones.lt, lovejob.lt, rekvi.lt) as surfaced by search. These aggregators disagree slightly by month and method (contract salary vs insured-employee averages). All "average salary" figures below are gross monthly EUR, company-wide (all roles), not finance-role-specific. Job-ad ranges are gross monthly EUR (Lithuanian law requires a pay range in ads).

National benchmarks for context:
- Sodra: average gross pay for full-month workers about 2,480 EUR (Q4 2025); finance and insurance sector about 3,000 EUR gross (2025) — [dirba.lt](https://www.dirba.lt/straipsniai/atlyginimai-lietuvoje-2026-minimali-alga-vidutinis-616.html); [argumentas.lt](https://argumentas.lt/lietuvos-darbo-rinka-2026-vidutinis-atlyginimas-1514-euru/)
- Q1 2026 average gross about 2,554 EUR; 2026 state VDU 2,312.15 EUR; minimum wage 1,153 EUR from 2026 — [finansistas.net](https://www.finansistas.net/vidutinis-darbo-uzmokestis.html); [auditum.lt](https://www.auditum.lt/index.php/atlyginimu-skaiciuokle/atlyginimo-skaiciuokle.html)
- August 2026 national average reported as 2,628 EUR — [TV3 / Verslo žinios](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/ispudingi-atlyginimai-valstybes-imonese-daugiau-nei-puse-darbuotoju-uzdirba-ir-5800-euru-n1555178)

## Q1. Global financial institutions' Vilnius/Kaunas hubs: which do front-office-adjacent / quant / risk / valuation / investment-ops work?

### Takeaway
The clearest high-value finance work in global hubs is quant risk/model validation at Danske Bank (Vilnius, ~4,400-4,600 staff), SEB and Swedbank, plus clearing-risk quant, issuer surveillance and financial-risk oversight at Nasdaq Vilnius (~550-600 staff, ~4,000-4,450 EUR avg). Western Union (~880-940 staff, ~3,300-3,600 EUR avg) is mostly operations/compliance; Barclays exited in 2019. I found no verified 2026 evidence of Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, MSCI or Mercer running finance teams in Lithuania.
