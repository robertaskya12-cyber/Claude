## Q1. Which foreign hedge funds, asset managers or trading firms have research/investment offices in Vilnius (Covalis Capital and similar)?

### Takeaway
Covalis Capital is the only foreign hedge fund I could confirm with investment staff in Vilnius. It has at least two equity analysts there, and it is founded and run by a Lithuanian-born CIO. I could not confirm any other foreign hedge fund research office in Vilnius. The other big "alternative investment" employers in Vilnius are fund administrators (Citco, Alter Domus, Amicorp) and market-infrastructure or ratings firms (Nasdaq, Moody's). Those are operations and middle-office roles, not investment roles.

## Q2. Which quant/prop/HFT/market-making firms (incl. crypto) have Lithuanian entities?

### Takeaway
Lithuania has a small but real home-grown prop/HFT scene. **Humbility, UAB** (crypto HFT and arbitrage, about 46-51 staff, Sodra average about EUR 6,000+/month) and **VNT Technologies, UAB / VNTrading** (quant research and algorithmic trading infrastructure, advertised base of EUR 5,000-10,000 gross/month plus bonus) are the stand-out hidden high payers. Quant trading roles also exist at the Ignitis energy-trading desk, at TransferGo's FX & Treasury desk, and in bank risk-quant teams (SEB, Swedbank, Artea). I found no Vilnius office for any major global HFT or crypto market maker.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Humbility and VNT are the two clear "hidden high payers" in Lithuanian quant trading. Humbility's Sodra averages of about EUR 6k+/month are roughly 2.5x the national average of EUR 2,416 gross (Mar 2026), and the profit-share months spike much higher. VNT's advertised EUR 5-10k base is the highest posted quant salary range I found in Lithuania.
- Both firms are tech/maths-heavy: they hire for olympiad, CS/maths and PhD profiles, and say finance experience is not required. A finance master's graduate would be competitive only with strong programming and statistics. Humbility's junior software engineer opening and VNT's researcher/Python roles show they do hire juniors.
- Ignitis, TransferGo and bank risk-quant roles pay EUR 3.3-5.7k gross. These are more accessible to a finance MSc graduate but are not "high-finance" prestige roles.

### Gaps
- No Sodra salary or headcount was obtained for Perpetual Trading Labs, and its line of business is unclear.
- Klaipėda: I found no quant/prop/hedge-fund employers (search budget ran out before a dedicated search).
- I could not check levels.fyi or Glassdoor for firm-specific comp at Humbility or VNT.
- Humbility's current 2026 open roles and any intern programme are unconfirmed.
- Other possible Lithuanian crypto prop/market-making UABs (e.g. found via Sodra data filtered for NACE 64.99/66.12 with high average pay) were not systematically screened. The next step is to use atvira.sodra.lt or scoris.lt "top salaries" filters.

## Q3. Which local hedge-fund-like or alternative investment funds exist (Bank of Lithuania-licensed AIF managers)?

### Takeaway
Lithuania's licensed AIF managers are mostly real estate, private equity and VC houses: INVL, Lords LB, Capitalica, Eika, Litcapital, Evernord and Aria Securities. True hedge funds are almost absent. The one notable algorithmic/CTA hedge fund, Algorithmic Trading Portfolio (managed under Orion Asset Management, run by Dr Aistis Raudys), reportedly stopped running around 2021.

### Cited Findings
- Bank of Lithuania register categories: "Valdymo įmonės, veikiančios pagal Alternatyviųjų kolektyvinio investavimo subjektų valdytojų įstatymą". Recent AIFM licences include **Evernord Asset Management UAB (2026-08-25)**, **UAB Aria Securities (2026-03-17)** and **UAB "Eika Asset Management" (2025-02-11)** — [lb.lt licences](https://www.lb.lt/lt/frd-licencijos); [lb.lt AIFM managers](https://www.lb.lt/lt/valdymo-imones-veikiancios-pagal-alternatyviuju-kolektyvinio-investavimo-subjektu-valdytoju-istatyma)
- INVL Asset Management UAB: AIFM licence from 2017-10-02 (LB000419) — [lb.lt](https://www.lb.lt/en/sfi-financial-market-participants/uab-invl-asset-management). It is launching a new INVL fund of up to EUR 200m to invest in CEE banks (Oct 2026) — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/09/3377823/0/lt/naujas-iki-200-mln-eur%C5%B3-dyd%C5%BEio-invl-fondas-investuos-%C4%AF-vidurio-ir-ryt%C5%B3-europos-bankus.html)
- Lords LB Asset Management UAB: licences LB000380 (2008, collective investment undertakings law) and LB000398 (2015, AIFM). Real estate and PE — [lb.lt](https://www.lb.lt/en/sfi-financial-market-participants/uab-lords-lb-asset-management); [lordslb.lt](https://lordslb.lt/en/)
- Capitalica Asset Management UAB: AIFM licence LB002140 since 2022-04-05; real-estate focus with Nasdaq-listed bonds — [lb.lt](https://www.lb.lt/en/sfi-financial-market-participants/uab-capitalica-asset-management); [capitalica.lt](https://capitalica.lt/en/about-us/)
- UAB "Litcapital Fund Management": management company for informed-investor funds since 2019-11-18, Jogailos g. 4, Vilnius — [lb.lt](https://www.lb.lt/en/sfi-financial-market-participants/uab-litcapital-fund-management). LitCapital itself is a PE firm investing in SMEs — [Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/fund-manager/litcapital/10492)

- **Algorithmic Trading Portfolio (ATP)**: described as "the first and the only algorithmic trading-based investment fund in Lithuania and the Baltics", an open-ended hedge fund for informed investors, supervised by the Bank of Lithuania, trading about 42 futures markets intraday. Launched in 2013 by Dr Aistis Raudys (Vilnius University) and managed under Orion Asset Management (2017 factsheet). One source says the fund stopped running in 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aistis_Raudys); [Orion AM factsheet 2017](http://am.orion.lt/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/FS_OAM_ATP_ENG_201707c.pdf); [algo.lt](https://www.algo.lt/en/); [Automaton Trading](http://automaton-trading.com/about-us.php)
- Lithuanian salary context: national average EUR 2,415.7 gross (Mar 2026); median gross EUR 1,934 — [tv3.lt](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/didziausi-atlyginimai-lietuvoje-nustebins-kova-sieke-net-117-tukst-euru-n1514886); [LRT](https://www.lrt.lt/naujienos/verslas/4/2849151/sodra-gyventojai-lietuvoje-vidutiniskai-uzdirba-1-514-euru-i-rankas)

### Inferences
- For a candidate who wants hedge-fund-style public-markets investing, the local AIF managers are mostly a PE/RE/VC route. (Local asset managers are presumably covered in more detail by another research stream.) Public-market investing roles in Lithuania are essentially Covalis (foreign) plus the bank and pension asset managers.
- Since ATP closed, there appears to be no active Lithuanian-licensed systematic hedge fund. This is unconfirmed: the register was not fully reviewed.

### Gaps
- The full Bank of Lithuania AIFM list could not be retrieved (lb.lt unreachable), so I could not screen every licensed manager for hedge-fund strategies. Orion Asset Management's and Synergy's current licence status was not confirmed.
- No Sodra average salaries were collected for INVL AM, Lords LB, Capitalica, Eika AM, Evernord or Aria Securities.

## Q4. What do Sodra average monthly gross salaries show?

### Takeaway
Among the firms I could check, the crypto HFT firm **Humbility (about EUR 6.1-6.3k/month regularly, spiking far higher in bonus months)** is the clearest "hidden high payer". VNT Technologies sits at about EUR 4.0-5.6k. Both are well above the national average of about EUR 2.4k. For comparison, the very top Sodra averages in Lithuania belong to tiny foreign tech units (Palantir Lithuania about EUR 51-63k, Talech Lithuania about EUR 117k in one month), which shows how volatile small-headcount averages are.

### Cited Findings
- Humbility, UAB (305825881): about EUR 6,100-6,300/month across late 2025 and early 2026 (EUR 6,182.78 in Jan 2026). Outlier of about EUR 40,318 in Apr 2026. EUR 9,269.64 2025 average on one site. 46-51 staff — [Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/305825881/darbuotojai_atlyginimai); [tax.lt](https://tax.lt/imones/255696-humbility-uab)
- VNT Technologies / VNTrading, UAB (306700560): EUR 4,578 (May 2026); EUR 5,573 (2025 average); EUR 4,017 (2026 YTD); 6-13 staff — [info.lt](https://www.info.lt/imones/VNT-technologies-UAB/2493986); [Scoris](https://scoris.lt/imone/306700560)
- Top Sodra averages nationally: Palantir Technologies Lithuania about EUR 62.8k (Jun 2026, 8 staff); Talech Lithuania EUR 117.4k (Mar 2026, 9 staff); Live Nation Lietuva EUR 91.7k (Mar 2026) — [77.lt](https://77.lt/paaiskejo-kur-lietuvoje-mokami-didziausi-atlyginimai-vienoje-imoneje-vidurkis-virsijo-62-tukst-euru); [tv3.lt](https://www.tv3.lt/naujiena/verslas/didziausi-atlyginimai-lietuvoje-nustebins-kova-sieke-net-117-tukst-euru-n1514886)
- Vilnius software engineer total comp median EUR 65,368/yr (levels.fyi, 94 data points), as a benchmark for tech-heavy quant roles — [levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/vilnius-ltu)
- SalaryExpert puts the Lithuania quant researcher average at about EUR 36.7k/yr base (survey estimate, low reliability) — [SalaryExpert](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/quantitative-researcher/lithuania)

### Inferences
- When screening Sodra data, firms with fewer than about 15 staff and averages above EUR 6k, registered under NACE 62.10 (programming) or 64.99/66.19 (finance) with no public product, are good candidates for hidden prop-trading shops. Humbility and VNT both register as "computer programming" despite being trading firms. Screening by finance codes alone would therefore miss them.

## Q5. How do they recruit, and do they hire fresh master's graduates?

### Takeaway
The Lithuanian quant/prop firms recruit off-campus, mainly through their own websites, LinkedIn, MeetFrank and quant job boards (Tradermath, QuantRoles). They target strong STEM, olympiad and Oxbridge profiles and explicitly accept candidates without finance experience. Covalis appears to hire Lithuanian analysts quietly through networks. I found no formal graduate or intern programmes at any of these firms in Lithuania.

### Cited Findings
- VNT Technologies advertises its roles on its own site. The researcher role does not require finance or trading experience and highlights Cambridge/Oxford, olympiad and PhD hires — [VNT](https://www.vnttechnologies.com/quantitative-researcher)
- Humbility recruits through its site, MeetFrank and LinkedIn (junior software engineer posting). It stresses a team from Cambridge, Imperial, Princeton and Oxford and pays annual profit sharing — [humbility.io](https://humbility.io/); [MeetFrank](https://meetfrank.com/company/humbility); [LinkedIn employee profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vismantas-stonkus-146b52140/)
- TransferGo's FX quant/trader ad asked for 3+ years' experience. Its MD also said he was seeking a junior/mid FX trader — [Flashpoint VC board](https://jobs.flashpointvc.com/companies/transfergo-2/jobs/53505591-fx-treasury-quantitative-analyst-trader); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kiveris/)
- Ignitis advertises on CVbankas, MeetFrank, Built In and its own careers page — [Ignitis careers](https://ignitisgrupe.lt/en/career/open-positions)
- Covalis's Vilnius analysts joined in 2021-2022 with no public job ads found (LinkedIn profiles only) — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rokas-jusys-2112471b2/)
- Lithuanian job ads must state pay, but formats vary: CVbankas shows both gross ("neatskaičius mokesčių") and net ("į rankas") — [CVbankas](https://www.cvbankas.lt/skelbimai-su-atlyginimais)

### Inferences
- For an HEC MSc International Finance graduate: Covalis (equity research, networking route), Humbility (if they have strong coding skills) and VNT (if they have strong maths/signal-processing skills) are the most "high-finance-like" Lithuanian options. Gravity Team in Riga is a nearby crypto market-maker alternative. All are small, so the realistic approach is a direct cold approach or referral rather than campus recruiting.
